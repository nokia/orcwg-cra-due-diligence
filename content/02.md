# To what do we apply due diligence

This chapter clarifies in which cases should a manufacturer execute an [article 13(5)][article-13] due diligence. 

## Which products

First and foremost [article 13(5)][article-13] due diligence is needed only for products placed to the market of the
European Union. If a product is not placed to the market of the European Union or the product is placed to the
market before 11 December 2027 and there are no substantial modifications (see [article 3(30)][article-3]) to the
product after, there is no need to do the due diligence.

Due diligence needs to be done during the development of all products and their remote data processing solutions. This
means that not only the software or hardware products, but also all remotely ran services which are needed to use the
products need to do due diligence.

## Which components of the product

Due diligence needs to be done for all third party components integrated to the product. However this should cover all
commercial, free and open source components this whitepaper has only the open source components in its scope.

## When to apply [article 13(5)][article-13] due diligence?

The [article 13(5)][article-13] due diligence should be applied when the component is integrated to the product during
the development of the product. When a products new, updated release is under development and the release contains
substantial modifications (see [article 3(30)][article-3]) the due diligence has to be repeated for all components
integrated to the product. [Article 13(5)][article-13] due diligence should be applied only during the development
process of the products during the integration of third party components.
